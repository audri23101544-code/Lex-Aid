LexAid is a comprehensive web-based platform designed to streamline and modernize legal aid services. It connects clients, lawyers, and administrators within a secure and efficient digital ecosystem, making access to justice faster, more organized, and more inclusive.

The platform features a multilingual interface to ensure accessibility for users from diverse linguistic backgrounds. It includes an automated eligibility (means test) system that evaluates whether clients qualify for legal aid based on predefined criteria.

LexAid allows users to upload and manage evidence with proper categorization and tagging, while a case status timeline and full audit log provide complete transparency of case progress. A secure in-app messaging system ensures confidential communication between clients and lawyers.

Additional features such as an anonymous legal forum, appointment scheduling, and an internal court calendar with conflict alerts enhance coordination and user engagement. The platform also supports automated legal document generation, time and expense tracking, and performance analytics dashboards for better operational insights.

Security is a core focus, with role-based access control (RBAC) and a secure evidence repository ensuring that sensitive data remains protected. Advanced tools like a conflict of interest checker, case priority scoring system, and emergency SOS intake form further improve decision-making and responsiveness.

Overall, LexAid aims to digitize legal workflows, improve efficiency, and provide equitable access to legal support through a modern, scalable solution.
